## Standards and scope

[eCH-0002 V1.0](https://www.ech.ch/de/ech/ech-0002/1.0) references ISO 15489 and has no UML or attribute dictionary in its eight-page PDF. [eCH-0039 V3.1](https://www.ech.ch/de/ech/ech-0039/3.1) contains the recalled diagram on page 37 (illustrative G0 model); pages 15–17 define Dossier and Dokument metadata. It is an exchange standard, not a mandatory complete catalog profile. The small local draft subset does not claim full eCH message/XML conformance, requiredness enforcement, operational document storage or document-instance membership modelling. Dateien describes file references and MIME types; payload storage remains outside this catalog.

Two separate **eCH Dokumentstatus / eCH Dossierstatus** lists each contain ten exact codes from the official V3.1 XSD. The XSD includes document status created, although the PDF's enumeration line omits it while its explanatory list includes it. Catalog status remains distinct from the lifecycle status described by these business attributes. KBOB Dokumenttyp is a local binding to an existing document-classification vocabulary, not an eCH-defined enumeration.

Registraturplan retains its stable name and identity; its definition identifies the BAR term **Ordnungssystem** and links to [BAR Geschäftsverwaltung](https://www.bar.admin.ch/de/geschaeftsverwaltung) and the official work aids. BAR guidance and approval responsibility are documented as sources; this does not transfer BBL ownership to BAR.
